## What the three passes add beyond the verdicts

- **F17 is stable, not noise.** Three boots, two times of day, 42.0–43.4 µs. The bisection
  (`latency_bisect.md`) stands. The correction is owed whatever is decided about optimising.
- **powercalc's default start is the figure to word carefully.** Pass 1 (long uptime) gave 2.29 s;
  the two fresh-boot passes gave 2.92 and 2.90 s. The median sits inside ±20% but only just, and the
  pass-to-pass spread is boot state, not code. §7.39's *conclusion*, that compiling at install makes
  confined starts faster, holds in every pass (−15%, −36%, −34%); its stated −24% is inside that
  range. Reporting a range is more honest than one figure.
- **Marginal memory's 134 MB in pass 2 was a single outlier**; pass 3 returned to 128 MB.
- **Not done, though §6 of the plan promised it:** p99 and a bootstrap 95% interval per figure. The
  verdicts above rest on medians across passes, as the register's criteria require; the interval is
  owed before phase R quotes a timing figure with a spread.

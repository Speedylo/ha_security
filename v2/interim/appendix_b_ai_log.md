Rows marked "rebuilt" were reconstructed from the project's records after the log was started on 23 October 2026; the others were recorded as they happened. The last column names the AI-use skill the occasion exercised: literacy (recognising how AI output fails), application (deciding when to use the AI and how to check it), ethics, and communication (stating truthfully who did what). The log is kept in the project's repository and continues to grow.
